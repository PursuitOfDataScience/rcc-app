I am a Computational Scientist at the University of Chicago. My research interests are data visualization, sentiment analysis, high-performance computing and machine learning. I have a strong background in Mathematics and Statistics, and this website is to showcase my data science portfolio and document my data science blogs/reports I work on the side besides my primary research. More importantly, as data science is still a nascent field and notions are forming rapidly, my blog posts are served as a way to document my learning process, in large part for sharing useful information and in small part for my own future reference. If you are interested in discussing any of my blog post or anything related to data science, please feel free to reach out to me. 

Thank you for visiting my website!
